## 📖 今日短文（考研英一阅读风格 · ~280 词）

### Should Governments Impose a Universal Basic Income?

The **prospect** of a universal basic income (UBI) has sparked **profound** debate across Western democracies. **Proponents** argue that a guaranteed monthly payment would **sustain** millions of low-wage workers through economic downturns, while critics **speculate** that it would **yield** mass idleness and erode the work ethic that **legitimate** economies depend upon.

The idea is not new. Pilot programs **confined** to small towns have produced mixed results: some show that **rational** adults, freed from survival pressure, invest in education and start businesses; others reveal that without **constraints**, a portion of recipients simply consume more. **Nonetheless**, the consensus among researchers is that early data, though limited in **perspective**, does not support the catastrophe scenario.

A deeper question is whether UBI addresses an **inherent** flaw in capitalism or merely **supplements** a broken system. Philosophers — whose **virtue** lies in asking uncomfortable questions — have long **elaborated** on this tension. If automation **obliges** society to rethink the link between labor and dignity, then a basic income may be not a compromise but a structural necessity. The government would need to **implement** it carefully, perhaps through a national **census** of household needs, ensuring that payments reflect actual living costs rather than **imposing** a flat sum that ignores regional variation.

In any case, advocates on both sides must move beyond speculation. What the public demands is not another transient experiment but a coherent, long-term commitment — one that does not diminish individual agency or neglect the most vulnerable.

## ⚠️ particular 专项强化（第 4 次出击）

这个词你从 Day 1 起连续标错。今天必须拿下。

**particular** /pəˈtɪkjələ(r)/

| 义项 | 释义 | 真题典型搭配 |
|------|------|-------------|
| ① 特定的 | = specific | this particular case / in particular |
| ② 详细的 | = detailed | give particulars of... |
| ③ 挑剔的 | = fussy | be particular about food |

**真题高频搭配**：
- **in particular** = 尤其，特别（副词短语，≠ especially 的口语感）
- **particular attention** = 特别关注
- **no particular reason** = 没有特别的原因

**辨析**：particular ≠ "尤其"（那是 especially/particularly 副词）。particular 本身是**形容词**，意思核心是"特定的那一个"。
