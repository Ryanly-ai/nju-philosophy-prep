## 📖 今日短文（考研英一阅读风格 · ~285 词）

### The Climate Policy Decade Ahead

Over the past **decade**, **liberal** democracies have struggled with one central question: how to turn scientific evidence about **climate** change into real policy. **Figures** released last month by the United Nations **indicate** that global emissions, far from declining, have risen by a **significant** margin — a finding that places governments and scientific **institutions** in open **conflict**.

The disagreement can be **segmented** into three layers. First, there is genuine debate about which **species** are most at risk and to what **degree**, a question on which biologists are rarely in accord. Second, even when the science is settled, lawmakers and citizens move at different speeds: lawmakers calculate **revenue** losses, while voters **reflect** on rising costs of **housing** and food. Third, the world's largest carbon emitters belong to no single **association**, so no body has the right to **charge** them on behalf of the rest.

Independent reviewers — the climate-policy equivalent of peer review — could narrow the gap. A small **association** of academics has proposed that every major emissions plan be evaluated by outside scientists before it reaches Congress. The reviewers would not vote, but their reports would be made public, allowing citizens to **conclude** for themselves whether a plan stands on solid ground, or is merely a **drawing** on the wall.

In short, the next decade will demand more than promises. Climate **assets** must be protected, fragile species restored, and the **brain** trust of every nation steered toward decisive action. Whether democracies rise to the moment — or let evidence be dimmed by political noise — will define the **conflict** of our time.

> 💡 **Day 1 错词锚点**（无加粗，自然嵌入复习）：peer review / evidence / Congress / in accord

### 隔日抽查 D-1 答案
1. **subject** (v.) /səbˈdʒekt/ 使服从；使遭受（subject X to Y）
2. **address** (v.) /əˈdres/ 处理，应对；致辞
3. **particular** 特定的；详细的；挑剔的
4. **comprehension** 理解力；领悟
5. **federal** 联邦的

### 测验答案
- **Q1**: figures / indicate / housing / significant / conflict / decade（**6/6** 才算掌握）
- **Q2**: **B** — `reflect on` = 思考、反省。A 是"反射"，C 是字面镜像，都不对。
- **Q3**: **C** — 起诉/控告（熟词僻义）。B "收费" 太具体，不符合 "right to charge them" 的法律语境。
- **Q4**: **A** — `in accord with` = 与…一致。**这是 Day 1 Q2 的错词回锅**，再答错就要专项强化了。
- **Q5**: 参考翻译（多种正确写法）：
  - *The **association**'s report **reflects** the **significant** threat this **species** is facing.*
  - *The **association**'s report **reflects** the major threat **species** are facing in this **decade**.*
  - 用上 association / reflect / significant / species（任 4 即可）
