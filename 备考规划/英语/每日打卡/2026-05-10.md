## 📖 今日短文（考研英一阅读风格 · ~270 词）

### Should Universities Abolish Standardized Admission Tests?

For decades, the SAT and similar exams have functioned as a **paradigm** of meritocratic admission — a single, ostensibly objective filter through which all applicants must pass. Recently, however, dozens of elite American universities have moved to **abolish** these tests, arguing that they **compel** disadvantaged students into a contest they cannot win. The debate is unusually **intricate** because both sides claim the authority of fairness.

Proponents of abolition note a persistent **discrepancy** between test scores and family income: wealthier students access expensive coaching, while **vulnerable** applicants face a **perpetual** disadvantage. They argue that the testing **hierarchy** has **deteriorated** into a marker of privilege rather than aptitude, and that admission committees can **distinguish** capable candidates through essays, interviews, and recommendations alone. From this perspective, abolishing tests would not diminish rigor but would **eliminate** a **redundant** gate that yields little new information.

Critics, nonetheless, **presume** that removing the SAT will merely shift the contest to terrain wealthier families navigate even better. Subjective judgments — essays, interviews — **fluctuate** with the reader's mood and **resemble** the very biases reformers claim to oppose. They are **reluctant** to accept that essays, often heavily edited by paid consultants, are more legitimate. **Indignation** has erupted, in particular, where families fear that without a numerical anchor, decisions may **coincide** with personal connections or institutional whim. In this particular respect, a test, however imperfect, is at least one thing the applicant cannot fully **manipulate**.

A rational path forward may not be to **abolish** but to supplement — pairing tests with rigorous context — so that scores **succeed** in their narrow purpose without imposing an inherent ceiling on those whom the system has long failed.

### particular 双义辨析答案
- 第一句：**副词短语 in particular** = 尤其，特别地（修饰整个句子，强调"愤怒在这种情境下尤其爆发"）
- 第二句：**形容词 particular** = 特定的（修饰名词 respect，"在这一特定方面"）

⚠ 真题陷阱：当 particular 单独修饰名词时是**形容词"特定的"**；当出现 in particular 时是**副词短语"尤其"**。两者都不是"详细的/挑剔的"。

### 测验答案
- **Q1**: discrepancy / vulnerable / deteriorated / perpetual / hierarchy / abolish
- **Q2**: **B** — fluctuate = rise and fall unpredictably（波动起伏）。A 是反义。
- **Q3**: **B** — 在...方面取得成功（succeed in doing/in sth）。"接替"用法是 succeed sb，本句是 succeed in 介词结构。
- **Q4**: **C** — resemble 是**及物动词**，直接接宾语：resemble one's father（×to）。这是真题高频陷阱。
- **Q5**: presume / abolish / compel / vulnerable / intricate / hierarchy
