## 📖 今日短文（考研英一阅读风格 · ~290 词）

### The Vague Promise of AI Regulation

Few subjects today are as **fundamental** to public debate as the **regulation** of artificial intelligence, yet the field remains stubbornly **vague**. **Advocates** of strict rules argue that current frameworks are **ambiguous** at best and **arbitrary** at worst — failing to **articulate** clear lines between research and harm. Critics, in turn, **allege** that hasty laws would **diminish** innovation and drive talent overseas. The conflict has not yet reflected real consensus, and trust between regulators and developers continues to erode.

Lawmakers have tried to **integrate** technical knowledge with traditional legal principles, but the gap is wide. A **coherent** framework, as experts indicate, must rest on three pillars: transparency, accountability, and reversibility. Yet scholars **depict** today's rules as a patchwork of **transient** measures — neither significant enough to prevent harm nor flexible enough to **bear** new technologies. The **prevalent** view in academic journals is that regulation, like the technology it polices, must evolve in real time.

Some governments have chosen to **retreat** from binding commitments, opting instead for voluntary codes that, while **plausible** on paper, are easy to **neglect** in practice. This approach, which subjects smaller firms to particular pressure while sparing dominant players, has been dismissed as another **arbitrary** compromise. A small association of former regulators has begun to **advocate** for international standards that no single nation can **surpass** or **prevail** over alone.

In the end, the question is not whether AI will reshape society — it already has — but whether public institutions can bear the regulatory burden without losing direction. Reflect on the past decade: we have addressed climate policy, charged corporations, and yet evidence of coordinated, decisive action remains thin.

### D-10 抽查答案
1. **particular** 特定的；详细的；挑剔的（**第三次盲区，再错就专项强化**）
2. **subject** (v.) /səbˈdʒekt/ 使服从；**使…遭受**（subject X to Y）
3. **address** (v.) 处理，应对；致辞（向…讲话）
4. **accord** /əˈkɔːd/ 一致；in accord with = 与…一致
5. **evidence** /ˈevɪdəns/ 证据；迹象
6. **reflect** (~on) 思考，反省（**不是抱怨**！）
7. **indicate** 指出；表明
8. **significant** 重大的；显著的

### 测验答案
- **Q1**: fundamental / regulation / vague / Advocates / allege / diminish（**6/6 才算掌握**）
- **Q2**: **B** — arbitrary = 任意的、武断的，没有合理根据。A 与之相反；C "balanced" 是中性，不准确。
- **Q3**: **B** — 使…遭受。`subject X to Y` 是真题考点搭配，**Day 1 D-1 抽查你写的"安排，主使"完全错**。
- **Q4**: **C** — 经典词性双发音。名词 /ˈædvəkət/ 重音前、尾音弱化为 /ət/；动词 /ˈædvəkeɪt/ 尾音强化为 /eɪt/。听说题常考。
- **Q5**: argue (or believe/think) / vague / regulations / diminish / trust（**5 空都对才行**，其中 vague/regulations/diminish 三个目标词必须用上）
